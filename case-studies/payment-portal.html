Organizational Web & Multi-Currency Payment Portal

Building a lightweight WordPress platform optimized for speed and readability, complete with KES and USD Paystack payment flows and custom domain email infrastructure.

WordPress Astra Theme Gutenberg Block Editor Paystack (KES/USD) Hostinger Mail

01 / Problem & Constraints

The organization needed a credible digital home capable of accepting contributions from both local supporters in Kenyan Shillings (KES) and international partners in US Dollars (USD), while avoiding bloated page builders that slow down mobile connections.

02 / Architecture & Stack Decisions

Deliberately chose the native Gutenberg block editor with the Astra theme over heavy visual builders to keep DOM size minimal and ensure non-technical administrators could publish updates safely.

[Visitor] → [Astra + Gutenberg Lightweight Frontend] → [Currency Selection: KES | USD] → [Paystack Checkout] → [Automated Receipt & Domain Follow-Up]

03 / Engineering & Implementation Highlights

  • Configured separate KES and USD Paystack payment endpoints so local mobile-money/card users and international cardholders experience frictionless checkout.
  • Provisioned custom domain mailboxes and email marketing templates to establish institutional trust and consistent supporter communication.
  • Built reusable Gutenberg block patterns so internal staff can publish announcements without breaking layout spacing or typography.

04 / Outcome

Launched a fast, accessible organizational platform supporting seamless local and international payments alongside unified domain email communications.
