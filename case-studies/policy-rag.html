Insurance Policy RAG Assistant

A Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) assistant engineered to query dense insurance policy documents and surface accurate, context-grounded answers.

Python RAG Architecture Text Chunking Vector Embeddings Prompt Grounding

01 / Problem & Constraints

Insurance policy documents are long, jargon-heavy, and tedious to search manually when verifying coverage limits, exclusions, or claim procedures. General LLMs hallucinate policy terms if queried without strict grounding in the exact policy text.

02 / Architecture & Stack Decisions

Implemented a Retrieval-Augmented Generation pipeline that separates document indexing from answer synthesis, forcing responses to rely exclusively on retrieved policy passages.

[Policy PDFs] → [Semantic Chunking + Embeddings] → [Vector Index]
[User Question] → [Similarity Retrieval (Top-K Clauses)] → [Grounded Prompt + LLM] → [Verified Policy Answer]

03 / Engineering & Implementation Highlights

  • Tuned document chunk sizes and overlap boundaries so multi-sentence exclusions and coverage conditions remain intact during retrieval.
  • Engineered system prompts instructing the model to decline speculation when a clause is absent from retrieved chunks.
  • Structured a clean modular pipeline so new policy documents can be ingested without rewriting query logic.

04 / Outcome

Reduced policy clause lookup from manual page-by-page scanning to natural-language queries backed by verifiable source passages.
